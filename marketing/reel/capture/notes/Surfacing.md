A humpback can stay down for most of an hour, though its ordinary dives are shorter. When it comes up, the first breath is loud enough to hear from the shore, and it's over in well under a second.

The blow is mostly air and seawater that sat in the blowhole on the way up. The lungs empty and fill again before the head goes back under, so the whole exchange happens in the time it takes the animal to roll forward.
