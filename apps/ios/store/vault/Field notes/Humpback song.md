Male humpbacks sing in the winter breeding grounds. A song is built from **units**, grouped into *phrases*, and phrases repeat as **themes**. Every male in one ocean sings roughly the ==same song==, and it drifts over a season.

> [!note] Why the songs change
> New themes spread east across the South Pacific, from one population to the next, within about two years. See [[Cultural transmission]].

## Frequency of a unit

A unit's pitch falls roughly as a power of its length:

$$
f(t) = f_0 \left( \frac{t}{t_0} \right)^{-\alpha}, \quad \alpha \approx 0.4
$$
