## Description (4000)

Gasp is a Markdown editor that opens instantly and gets out of the way. Type a few stars or a pound sign and your words turn bold, italic or into a heading right where you're writing. Tap a line to see the Markdown behind it, and it tidies up again when you move on.

Every note is an ordinary .md file in a folder you own. You'll find them in the Files app, you can open them in any other Markdown app, and nothing is locked inside Gasp.

Write the way you think:
• Headings, lists, checklists, quotes, callouts and code
• Tables you edit in place
• Math written in LaTeX, drawn as real equations
• Links between notes with [[double brackets]], and backlinks that show which notes point at the one you're reading
• A note embedded in another with ![[its name]]

Find your way around:
• A sidebar with your folders, the note's outline, its links and your tags
• Search across every note
• Tabs, so the notes you're working on stay one swipe away
• Light and dark themes that follow your phone

Keep your phone and computer in step:
• Sync through iCloud Drive with nothing to set up
• Or sync through your own GitHub repository, for free, with every change in its history
• Gasp for Mac opens the same folder, so you can pick up where you left off

Gasp sends one anonymous message a day (the app's version, the iOS version and the chip type) so we can count how many people use it. It never sends your notes, their names or anything about you, and you can turn it off in Settings. The full details are at gaspmd.com/privacy.
