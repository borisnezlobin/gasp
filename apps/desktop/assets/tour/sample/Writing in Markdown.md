Markdown is a way of formatting text with ordinary characters. Two stars make **bold**, one star makes *italics*, and two equals signs make ==a highlight==. Backticks mark `code`.

## Headings

A line that starts with `#` is a heading. More `#`s make a smaller one.

## Lists

- A dash starts a bullet.
	- Press Tab to nest one under another.
1. A number and a full stop start a numbered list.
- [ ] Square brackets make a task.
- [x] An x inside them ticks it.

## Quotes and callouts

> A line that starts with `>` is a quote.

> [!tip] Callouts
> A quote that starts with `[!tip]`, `[!note]` or `[!warning]` becomes a callout.
