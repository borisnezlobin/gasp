App icon: humpback whale

The whale in the app icon is an ink-style render of a rigged humpback model
derived from the following source geometry.

Asset: Megaptera_FULL.stp
Authors: Susana Gutarra Díaz, Thomas L. Stubbs, Benjamin C. Moon,
Colin Palmer, and Michael J. Benton
Publication: Large size in aquatic tetrapods compensates for high drag
caused by extreme body proportions
Publication DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s42003-022-03322-y
Source archive: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.5979631
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
License URL: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes to the source geometry: it was tessellated from NURBS to triangles,
reoriented, scaled to a 14-meter representative length, adjusted at the
pectoral fins and head, made more robust through the torso, given modeled
ventral throat pleats, rigged, weighted and animated. For the icon it was
posed mid-breach, rendered in an ink style, rotated, faded where it sits
below the text lines, and composited onto a paper-coloured tile.

No endorsement by the original authors is implied.
